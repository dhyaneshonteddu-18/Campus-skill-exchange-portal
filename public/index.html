How It Works

1. Sign Up

Create your account and build your profile with your details and initial skills.

2. Add Skills

Add the skills you can teach and provide descriptions of your expertise.

3. Find & Connect

Search for skills you want to learn and send requests to students offering them.

4. Learn Together

Accept connections, share materials, and grow through peer-to-peer learning.
